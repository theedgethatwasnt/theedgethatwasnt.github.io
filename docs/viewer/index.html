3,000 bars of EUR/JPY M5 with the widest toolset of the three: a full bank of togglable overlay and sub-panel indicators, click-to-toggle with right-click parameter editing.

EUR/GBP M5 price with a curated indicator set including the Aroon oscillator, stacked in separate panels beneath the candles.
