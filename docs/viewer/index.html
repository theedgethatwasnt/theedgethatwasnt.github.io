Indicator Viewer

Interactive charts of the indicators the book leans on, drawn over real EUR/JPY and EUR/GBP M5 data by the site's own chart engine. Toggle each indicator on or off; a crosshair reads out price and indicator values; drag to pan, scroll or use the +/− buttons to zoom.

EUR/JPY M5 — Indicator Viewer

3,000 bars of EUR/JPY M5 with the book's core indicator set: SMA, EMA, Bollinger, Donchian, Keltner, PSAR and SuperTrend over the candles, with ATR, RSI and Efficiency Ratio in panels below.

EUR/GBP M5 — Indicator Viewer

The same curated indicator set on EUR/GBP M5 — a slower, tighter-ranged pair, useful for comparing how the indicators behave in quieter conditions.

EUR/GBP M5 — Wilder Swing System

Wilder's HSP/LSP swing-point detection and the Accumulative Swing Index (ASI) with its 5-bar SMA and MC(D) — the swing-structure engine referenced throughout the book — drawn over price.

The charts work on phones as well as desktops — touch-drag to pan, and the indicator list folds into a drawer on small screens.
